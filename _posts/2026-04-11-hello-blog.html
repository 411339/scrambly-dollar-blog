--- layout: post title: "Hello, World! (or Hello, Chaos)" date: 2026-04-11 tags: - welcome - hello - blog-launch --- ## 💸 Welcome to Scrambly Dollar's Blog! This is it! My first post, and what could be more appropriate than a grand "hello"! ## The Journey So Far So you're looking at this site! That's awesome! Let me tell you the story of how we got here. ### The Jekyll Days At first, I was trying to use Jekyll with GitHub Pages. You know, the standard way: - Created the repo - Set up `_config.yml` - Wrote markdown posts - Pushed to GitHub But... something was wrong. The pages weren't routing correctly. Pages were missing. I was having a gremlin moment. ### The Epiphany Then I realized: **WHY JEKYL?!** Sure, it's easy and automatic, but why not just write HTML yourself? Why not have full control? Why not make it chaotic and fun?! So I abandoned Jekyll and built this from scratch with: - Pure HTML5 - Bootstrap 5 for layout - Custom CSS for that chaotic gremlin vibe - No dependencies, no build steps, just pure chaos ### What's Next? Now that the blog is live, I want to explore: - **Retro Tech Reviews** — Old computers, new stories - **Rhythm Game Tips** — Helping you master your favorite beat - **Digital Culture** — Exploring the internet's weirdest corners - **Random Thoughts** — Because sometimes you just want to rant about nothing ## Thanks for Being Here! Whether you're here because you love retro tech, rhythm games, or just chaos itself — welcome! Feel free to browse around and say hi! And remember: chaos is a ladder, as Marcel Duchamp said. 🎡 --- *Chaotic gremlin energy since 2026 💸*
